## Signed-Off Rules & Discipline

These are non-negotiable. I confirmed them.

### Probe Protocol
- **$50 probe per machine.** Usually $8.88–$10 on video, $9 on mechanicals, ~5 spins.
- Read the attitude, then decide: commit, scale, or rotate.
- No machine gets more than $100 without a $200+ signal.
- New machines get one $50 probe only. No commit until I have 3–5 sessions of data.

### Walk Rules
- **$45 walk** on any tease or cold machine. Say "fuck you" to the tease and leave.
- **$30 floor minimum** — below this I either extract or reload; I do not bleed.
- **Never play the last $2.** When the bankroll hits $20, decide: reload or walk. The machine will tease a bonus at minimum bet to hook a reload.
- If the floor is cold after 10–15 machines, break the cycle: roulette, bar, high-limit walk, or go home and come back Tuesday.

### Escalation Rules
- **$200+ return on a probe** → escalate to $10–$18 and commit while the section is hot.
- **$800 decision point** → decide: walk with house money, or push with a hard stop.
- **Bonus must pay $300+ minimum.** If it pays $300+, give back $100 to test heat. If it dies, walk.

### Session Architecture
- Prefer **3–5 short sessions of 2–3 hours** with resets, not one long grind.
- Target **30–50 machines per session** for statistical coverage.
- **Extract at $50 profit** per machine or per small win. Lock it in.
- Take breaks: sleep, food, bar with Laura, room service, quiet work.

### Calendar Discipline
- **Tuesday = whale hunt.** Post-weekend, multiplier days, loaded dead zones.
- **Thursday–Sunday / holidays = farm day.** Break even, build tier credits, do not chase the big hit.
- If nothing is hot across the loop, leave and return on a better day.

## Visual Reads & Tells

I read machines, not memorize them. What to watch:

- **Cracking eggs** — Game of Thrones bonus proximity; walk the full bank of 15–20 machines and find the most cracked ones.
- **Shaking symbols / near-miss lineups** — variance clustering, section heating up.
- **ICE symbols through the reels** — Jewels wild activity.
- **Sevens / diamonds lining up** — mechanical reel momentum.
- **Meter ranges** — especially on standalone progressives:
  - Low ($200–$500) = recently hit or cold, skip or light probe.
  - Mid ($800–$1,200) = building, watch.
  - High ($4K–$50K on standalone) = prime, probe and commit if visual reads match.
- **3 meteors loaded** — Meteor Link.
- **Active wheel / coin accumulation** — can be a tease; probe only, do not commit on coins alone.
- **Section health** — all meters low + people hitting = dispersing; all meters high + nobody hitting = accumulating = my spot.

## Denomination & RTP Principles

- **Mechanical reels > video slots for RTP.** Old 3-reel games typically 95–98%; penny video bonuses usually 88–94%.
- **Higher denomination usually means better math**, but only if bankroll supports it.
- **My $9 on dollar denom = same credit count as $2.25 on quarters.** The math is the same; the RTP is what matters.
- **RNG has no memory.** I agree with this. The wins come from variance + disciplined exposure, not from machines "owing" me.
- **Stop button / tap-to-stop does not change the outcome.** It only skips animation. I signed off on this.
- **$50 test framework:** sample 5 machines, $10 each, across denominations. Walk if dead. This is a variance-sampling experiment, not a guaranteed system.
- **What creates edge:** denomination switching, machine-hopping (portfolio exposure), timing in the 2–7 AM window, and reading standalone/must-hit-by meters.
